About

A bit about how I got here.

I'm a final-year Software Engineering student at the Adventist University of Central Africa (AUCA), working toward a Bachelor of Information Technology with Honours. My path into this field started back in secondary school, where I specialized in Software Development, and it's carried through into university with a growing focus on full-stack web development, databases, and software testing.

Most of my time goes into building things end to end — from the interface down to the database layer — and figuring out how the pieces fit together. I like problems that force me to slow down and reason through them properly rather than guess: I'll usually break a problem into smaller pieces, test each part as I go, and only trust a solution once I've tried to break it myself.

Outside of coursework, I take on freelance web projects for clients, and I'm currently building an adaptive traffic-light simulation as my final-year project. When I'm not at a keyboard, I follow Arsenal, spend time around cars, and try to get outdoors — hiking and traveling when I can.
